Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

This policy explains what Brahvia AI ("Brahvia", "we") collects, why, who helps us process it, and the choices you have. We try to keep it short and plain. Brahvia is a small independent project.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and name. If you use Google sign-in, we receive the basic profile Google shares (name and email). We never see your password; sign-in is handled by our authentication provider.
  • What you create: your chat messages, build requests, generated project files, saved versions and files you attach. We need these to show your work back to you.
  • Credits: your credit balance and a history of what each request cost.
  • Memory notes: if memory is on, a few short notes about you (for example your business name). You can see and delete them in Settings.
  • GitHub connection (optional): if you connect GitHub, we store your GitHub username and an access token, kept encrypted on our server. We also record where you published a project.
  • Share links: if you create one, the shared version of that project can be opened by anyone who has the link.
  • In your browser: your sign-in session and small preferences such as light or dark theme.

We do not run advertising, we do not use analytics trackers, and we do not sell your data.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and server functions.
  • Google Gemini writes the AI answers. Your messages, build requests and attached files are sent to Gemini. Brahvia currently uses the free (unpaid) Gemini API quota. Under Google's terms for unpaid services, Google may use submitted content and responses to improve its products and machine learning technologies, and human reviewers may read them after the data is disconnected from your account. Do not enter passwords, secrets or sensitive personal information into Brahvia. If we move to Google's paid service, this paragraph will be updated.
  • GitHub Pages serves this website. If you connect GitHub, Brahvia calls GitHub on your behalf to publish.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver fonts and a code library to your browser, so those services see your IP address when the page loads.

How long we keep it, and your choices

  • You can delete a project or a chat at any time. Memory notes can be deleted one by one, cleared, or switched off in Settings.
  • You can download a copy of your data from Account.
  • If you delete your account, we delete your profile, projects, chats, credits, memory notes and GitHub connection. Sites you already published to your own GitHub stay there; you control those.
  • Backups held by our hosting providers may take a little time to expire.

Security

Data is stored with access rules so each person can read only their own projects, chats and credits. GitHub tokens are stored encrypted. The AI key is kept on the server and never sent to your browser. No online service can promise perfect security, so please use a strong, unique password.

Children

Brahvia is meant for people aged 18 and over. If you are younger, please use it only with a parent or guardian's permission.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the date above and, where it matters, tell you in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: open an issue on our GitHub page. Please do not post private information there.
